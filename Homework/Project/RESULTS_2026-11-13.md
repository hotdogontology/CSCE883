## Initial example inspection (not a complete annotation)

Inspected the first three rejected examples and one accepted example in generation
order. In herald-43725 (Set.offDiag_insert), generated English incorrectly writes
the off-diagonal of a union as only the union of the two off-diagonals, omitting
cross-product terms supplied in the source statement and original English. Later
steps reintroduce the terms without a sound explanation. Judge decision: mismatch.

In herald-11044 (Equiv.Perm.Disjoint.symm), the generated English describes
disjointness as pointwise inequality of permutations, whereas the original
English describes the fixed-point disjunction and Lean explicitly rewrites using
or_comm. Judge decision: mismatch. This is a concrete translation concern, not
evidence that the source formalization is wrong.

The inspected accepted example, herald-40874 (CochainComplex.of_d_ne), explains
selection of the zero branch when i+1 differs from j, consistent with the visible
Lean proof's dsimp and dif_neg steps. This single observation does not verify all
49 accepted outputs.
