# Project references and resources

## Agda datasets

- [Agda-Stdlib — phanerozoic](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phanerozoic/Agda-Stdlib): Dataset of formal statements and proofs from the Agda standard library, with names, imports, dependencies, and source metadata. Candidate source of Agda examples; English/formal correspondence labels would need to be supplied and reviewed separately.
- [Agda-Cubical — phanerozoic](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phanerozoic/Agda-Cubical): Dataset of formal statements and proofs from the Cubical Agda library, with names, imports, dependencies, and source metadata. Another candidate source for the Agda extension of the project.

## Natural-language/formal-language resources

- [Informath — Grammatical Framework](https://github.com/GrammaticalFramework/informath): Mathematical terms, definitions, and propositions across natural and formal languages, including English and Agda. Potential source of corresponding statements and controlled examples for the correspondence checker.

## Related research

- **Pei Huang (2025). _Autoformalization for Agda via Fine-tuning Large Language Models_.** Master's thesis, Chalmers University of Technology and University of Gothenburg. [Full thesis (PDF)](https://odr.chalmers.se/server/api/core/bitstreams/f68aff4a-3865-41b0-ac54-2ce3d917e783/content). Introduces the synthetic multilingual SMAD corpus generated through Informath and studies fine-tuning for Agda autoformalization. Relevant background for data generation and transfer across natural and formal languages.
