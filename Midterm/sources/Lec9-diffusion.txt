     ○   Vanishing gradients: if discriminator is too good, gradients go to zero
     ○   Mode collapse: if the generator learns to generate an exceptionally
         plausible output, it will just continue generating it. Then the discriminator
         will learn to always reject it, and then the generator will produce the same
         outputs, which the discriminator will then reject… bad loop!
